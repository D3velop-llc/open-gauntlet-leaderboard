How to read this section

None of this is benchmarked here. OpenGauntlet measures Conversational Language Humanlikeness — transcript-level qualities like word choice, warmth and emotional reasoning. It deliberately does not score prosody, intonation or timing. This section is a sourced survey of the speech market, not a trial. Where a claim could not be verified against a primary source, it says so rather than smoothing it over.
